Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Synora AI ("we", "us") provides an AI-powered adaptive study scheduler. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

When you use the app you provide: your email/phone, study profile details (exam, target level, weekly hours, school/college), tasks and subjects, energy reports, quiz responses, comments on community posts, ideas, and feedback you submit.

How We Use Data

  • To generate, adapt and reschedule your personalized study plan.
  • To show you your own study statistics and insights on your profile.
  • To power the community (posts, comments, ideas) and moderation.
  • To serve AI assessments through our AI provider.

AI Processing

Plan generation and quiz features call a third-party Gemini API using your task/subject data. Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Storage

Your data is stored securely in our hosting provider's database. We do not sell your data. Sessions are kept in signed cookies; you are logged in by default as a guest until you create an account, and you can sign out or clear cookies to reset the guest session.

Contacts

For privacy questions please use the contact link on the site.
