Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

When you create an account we store your name, email address, and a securely hashed password. As you use Synora we store the study information you choose to add: tasks, timetable entries, exam plans, quiz results, focus sessions, energy check-ins, and community posts you publish.

How we use it

Your data is used to run the features you ask for — generating schedules, adapting your plan when tasks are missed, tracking progress, and showing your own analytics. We do not sell your personal data.

AI processing

Some features send the text you provide (for example a syllabus excerpt or a quiz request) to our AI provider so it can generate a response. Only the content needed for that request is sent. Do not paste sensitive personal information into AI prompts.

Cookies and sessions

We use a session cookie to keep you signed in and to remember your theme preference. The cookie is HTTP-only and is not used for advertising. You can clear it at any time by logging out or clearing your browser storage.

Storage and security

Data is stored in our application database. Passwords are hashed, not stored in plain text, and access is limited to the systems that need it. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please use a unique password.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, change your password, and delete community posts you created. To request deletion of your account and associated study data, contact us at the address below.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at privacy@synora.app.

This policy describes the current Synora demo application and may be updated as the product evolves.
